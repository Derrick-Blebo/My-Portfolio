My
Story.

I learned architecture before I learned design. It started at the University of Ghana, analyzing political systems and organizing information structures. Systems only work when every element serves a precise purpose. My job was to find the logic before crafting the visual.

Now at EV Afriq, I lead integrated marketing and lead-generation architecture to drive the regional shift toward electric mobility. From campaign strategy to full visual identity across real estate, construction, and corporate sectors, the goal remains unchanged: build systems that scale brands, engage markets, and last. Multiple roles. One constant principle: decode the system. Then design the authority.
